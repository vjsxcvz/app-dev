# 🏀 Uncle Drew

## Overview
Uncle Drew is a 2018 sports comedy film about an old basketball legend who returns to the court.  
It combines humor, heart, and basketball in one fun story.

---

## Main Cast
- 🧓 Kyrie Irving as Uncle Drew  
- 😂 Lil Rel Howery as Dax  
- 🕶️ Shaquille O’Neal as Big Fella  
- 🎧 Chris Webber as Preacher  
- 💃 Tiffany Haddish as Jess  

---

## Plot Summary
> “You don’t stop playing because you get old.  
> You get old because you stop playing.”  
> — Uncle Drew

After losing his basketball team, Dax meets Uncle Drew, a wise old streetball legend.  
Together, they gather Drew’s old teammates to compete in a big tournament and prove that age is just a number.

---

### Key Themes
1. Teamwork – Success comes when everyone works together.  
2. Perseverance – Never give up, no matter your age.  
3. Friendship – Bonds are stronger than trophies.
